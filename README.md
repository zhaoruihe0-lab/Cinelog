CineLog — A Personal Film Archive

CineLog is a personal film archive built around privacy and independent curation.

It provides a private space to record personal film ratings and viewing experiences, while allowing users to curate their collections through custom covers, posters, and selected film stills. Multiple browsing modes offer different ways to explore the archive, while integrated reviews, viewing notes, and external references support deeper engagement with cinema.

Rather than following a standardised platform format, CineLog reflects an individual approach to organising, presenting, and interpreting films.

Key features

* Multiple browsing modes for exploring a personal film collection.
* Custom film covers, posters, and still-image galleries.
* Personal ratings, viewing notes, and reviews.
* Search, filtering, and collection statistics.
* Data export and restoration.

Tech stack: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and IndexedDB.
